# Announcement film: three storylines

The one thing: "Nobody can build the Earth. Anyone can build a door."
Assumptions: 28 to 35 s, 16:9 1920x1080, music and sound effects, no voiceover, nothing paid, end-card ask left as a placeholder.
Status: waiting for Justin to pick. Nothing is built.

## B. The missing piece (recommended)

Device: the result first. Form: an endless pull-back in one take.
A finished street at dusk, in 3D, with one object missing: a dashed outline where a street light should be, tagged "Open task". Someone claims it, the part drops in and the street lights up; the camera pulls back and the whole planet is covered in the same dashed outlines.

- First 2 s: a warm, detailed street corner fills the frame. One dashed outline pulses; the tag reads "Street light: open task".
- Beats: street with a gap -> "Claimed" -> the part builds and snaps in at its connector, the light turns on -> pull back: block, district, city, each full of outlines filling in -> globe, mostly empty -> claim line -> end card.
- Look: dusk blue ground, warm sodium light as the only accent, solid shaded 3D parts, dashed white outlines for what is missing.
- Sound: slow piano and strings that grow; a solid click for each part, a hum when the light comes on.

## A. One evening, one part

Device: one object across the system. Form: object-to-object chain.
Follows a single car door from a task card, through a contributor's AI building it, the checks passing, the merge, then snapping onto a car that drives into a street that tiles across a real map.

- First 2 s: a task card flips over on a bright ground: "Car door, front left. 48 hours." A hand-sized wireframe door rises out of it.
- Beats: task card -> door builds -> checks tick (scale, connectors, triangles) -> merged -> door snaps to car -> car parks in street -> street repeats along a real street layout -> city -> end card.
- Look: bright off-white studio, clay-grey parts, one signal-orange accent for connectors.
- Sound: light percussive build, one tick per check, a door thunk on the snap.

## C. Too big (the bold one)

Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.
Huge kinetic type, where every letter is made of real street layouts from OpenStreetMap. Hard cuts on the beat: "THE EARTH IS TOO BIG" / "FOR ONE STUDIO" / "SO BUILD ONE DOOR". The letters break into parts that reassemble as a car, a block, a globe.

- First 2 s: the word EARTH fills the frame, its letters cut from a real city street grid, scrolling fast.
- Beats: EARTH -> TOO BIG (the frame overflows) -> FOR ONE STUDIO -> silence, one small door -> BUILD ONE DOOR -> doors, wheels, windows flood in on the beat -> globe -> end card.
- Look: black and white map linework, one colour field per cut, a heavy grotesque typeface. Needs an OpenStreetMap credit on the end card.
- Sound: hard drums, cuts on every half beat, one bar of silence before the door.
